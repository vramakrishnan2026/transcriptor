Transcripter

An extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers that saves the full transcript of any Teams meeting recording you can view as a clean text file, with speakers and timestamps. It can also build a key-moments report, with a screenshot from the recording at each important moment.

Why this exists

You can read the transcript, but often you can't download it, and the panel only shows a few lines at a time. So getting the whole meeting out means copying it piece by piece.

Install it (about two minutes)

  1. Download and extract the zip

    Get Transcripter.zip from the same folder as this guide. Right-click it and choose Extract All. You'll get a folder containing transcripter.

  2. Open the extensions page

    Type your browser's address below into the address bar and press Enter.

    Chromechrome://extensions
    Microsoft Edgeedge://extensions
    Bravebrave://extensions
    Operaopera://extensions
    Vivaldivivaldi://extensions

    Using another Chromium-based browser? Try chrome://extensions; most of them redirect it to their own extensions page.

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    Look for the Developer mode switch. It's in the top-right corner in most browsers, and in the left sidebar in Microsoft Edge. This lets you install extensions from a folder.

  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder

    Select the transcripter folder you extracted (the one that contains manifest.json). Transcripter appears in the list, and a welcome page opens.

  5. Pin it to your toolbar

    Open the extensions menu in the toolbar (a puzzle-piece icon in most browsers) and pin Transcripter. In Edge, click the eye icon next to it to show it in the toolbar. Clicking the VR icon opens Transcripter in a side panel next to the page (a small popup in Opera and Vivaldi).

Keep the extracted folder. The browser loads Transcripter from it, so moving or deleting the folder removes the extension. A permanent spot like Documents works well.

Updating. To install a newer version, replace the folder's contents, then click the refresh icon on Transcripter's card on your browser's extensions page.

Export a transcript

  1. Open the meeting recording in your browser

    Recordings open on a SharePoint page. If Teams opens it in the desktop app, use Open in browser. Make sure you're in the browser where Transcripter is installed.

    Tip: press play for a moment and let the page finish loading before you export. Transcripter pauses the video for you.

  2. Click the VR icon in the toolbar

    The side panel opens, finds the meeting, and shows its title, date, and number of lines. If the Transcript panel is closed, it opens it for you. The side panel follows the tab you're on, so switching to another recording updates it automatically.

  3. Choose your options and click Export transcript

    Text works everywhere; Markdown is handy for notes apps. Turn timestamps off for a cleaner read. Your choices are remembered.

    Only need part of the meeting? Enter a Time range in mm:ss, such as 12:00 to 20:00. Leave either box empty to start from the beginning or run to the end.

  4. Leave the recording tab alone while it works

    The video pauses and the transcript scrolls by itself. The side panel and a badge in the corner of the page show progress. The export keeps going even if you close the panel. Need it sooner? Click Stop and save to download everything collected so far; the file is marked as partial.

  5. Find the file in your Downloads

    It's named after the meeting and its date. The panel shows a preview, and lets you copy the text or save it once more.

Find key moments, with screenshots

After an export, the panel can help you pull out the moments that matter, with a picture of what was on screen at each one. Handy when a slide, drawing or demo was shared and the words alone don't tell the story.

  1. Click Copy AI prompt

    This copies the transcript together with instructions asking the AI to list the key moments, one per line, as mm:ss | Title | Why it matters. It looks out especially for moments where something was shown on screen.

  2. Paste it into your AI assistant

    Use whichever assistant you're allowed to use for meeting content. Copy its reply.

  3. Paste the reply into the panel

    Transcripter reads the timestamps and shows how many moments it found. It understands most formats, including numbered lists and bullets.

  4. Click Capture screenshots

    The video jumps to each moment and Transcripter grabs the frame. Keep the recording tab in front while it works; the video goes back to where it was at the end.

  5. Open the report or save it

    Open report shows each moment with its screenshot, the AI's reason, and the transcript lines around it. Click Save as PDF at the top of the report, or use Save as HTML to keep a single file you can share.

Good to know

Private by design. Transcripter works only on SharePoint recording pages and never sends anything anywhere. Everything happens in your browser. The AI prompt is copied to your clipboard; you decide where to paste it.

Only what you can already see. It exports transcripts you have access to view. Follow your team's rules for sharing meeting content.

Copying to the clipboard. Windows clipboard history and sync can keep copied text. For sensitive meetings, use the downloaded file instead.

If something goes wrong

"No transcript found"
The recording may not have a transcript, or the panel didn't open. Click Transcript on the right side of the player, then Check again.
"Some lines may be missing"
Export again. Keep the recording tab visible and don't scroll the transcript while it runs.
"Transcripter wasn't allowed to capture the screen"
Some recordings protect their video frames, so Transcripter captures the visible tab instead. That needs permission the browser gives when you click the VR icon. Click it while the recording tab is open, then capture again.
A screenshot is black or shows the wrong thing
The screen may have been switching at that moment. Edit the time in the AI's reply to a few seconds later and capture again.
It stopped working after a Teams update
Microsoft occasionally changes the recording page. Let Vinay know so the extension can be updated.
